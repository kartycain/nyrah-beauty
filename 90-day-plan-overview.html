Client dependency

The seven client confirmation questions in Phase 2 are the single biggest risk to the timeline. Product pages
All 37 — same standard, no exceptions
The previous plan said "top priority product pages." This plan says all 37. Every page a customer or AI engine lands on must be complete. A brilliant Infuse FF page does zero work if the customer then clicks to Absorb and finds no FAQ, no schema, and stale copy. Consistency is the signal. Inconsistency is noise.
Blog pace
Two blogs per week — not more
Publishing faster on a new domain delivers no compounding benefit. Two per week gives Google time to crawl, index, and associate the domain with the topic cluster before the next layer arrives. The goal is a knowledge domain, not a content dump.
Absolute rule
No blogs until Phases 1–4 are complete
Do not publish a single blog until: all critical regulatory fixes are live, all 37 product pages are rewritten and schema-complete, all 7 new foundation pages are published and interlinked. A blog linking to a broken product page, a page with a regulatory error, or a page with no FAQ block wastes every hour spent writing it. The foundation is not a nice-to-have. It is the only thing that makes the content work.
