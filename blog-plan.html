Section 01

Topical Authority Path

Five layers published in sequence. Each layer must be established before the next is opened. This is not a complexity ladder — it is a coverage sequence. You are teaching Google what knowledge domain Nyrah owns.

01 Weeks 1–3
Establish the primary entity Teach Google what Nyrah is — before anything else
  • What is hormonal skin change? — Defines the primary problem domain. Google reads this first to understand what Nyrah is about. Without this, every subsequent blog on hormonal topics has no authority anchor.
  • How does a skincare routine work for sensitive skin? — Defines the solution domain. Establishes that Nyrah occupies the skincare-for-sensitive-skin knowledge space.
  • What does fragrance-free mean in skincare? — Defines Nyrah's primary product attribute. This is the foundational claim that every fragrance-free product on the site depends on being credible.
  • What is the skin barrier and why does it matter? — Defines the functional frame for all Nyrah products. Every product in the range addresses the skin barrier in some way. This blog is the semantic root.
02 Weeks 3–5
Establish the primary ingredient entities Associate Nyrah with its proprietary ingredient technologies
  • What is hyaluronic acid and what do different molecular weights do? — Establishes Drench / PrimalHyal™ 3K authority. Generic HA content exists everywhere. Nyrah's claim is triple molecular weight — that specificity is the differentiator.
  • What is Bakuchiol and how does it compare to retinol? — Establishes Transform authority. One of the highest-volume ingredient queries in skincare 2024–2026. This blog must be published before the comparison blog — foundational before specific.
  • What is Niacinamide and why is it in moisturisers for hormonal skin? — Establishes Infuse authority. The intersection of niacinamide + hormonal skin is Nyrah's specific angle — not the generic niacinamide explainer that already exists on every beauty site.
  • What is Astaxanthin and why is it used in SPF? — Establishes Lumina SPF30 authority. Astaxanthin is an underexplained ingredient in SPF products. Nyrah's 6,000× antioxidant claim needs a content anchor before it can be cited.
03 Weeks 5–8
Establish the skin concern entities Own the problem spaces Nyrah's products solve
  • How to care for perimenopausal skin — Highest-value cluster for Nyrah's primary audience. This is the highest-intent query from the brand's core demographic. It must be anchored in Layer 3, not introduced cold in Layer 1.
  • What causes hormonal acne in women over 30, and how is it different from teenage acne? — Differentiation content. The question modifier "different from teenage acne" is what makes this rankable — it is specific enough to target without competing with every acne article on the web.
  • Rosacea triggers in skincare ingredients: what to avoid — Reinforces the Rosacea Relief Bundle positioning. This is also the page that protects the Transform product — by establishing what triggers rosacea, Nyrah demonstrates it understands the condition it is selling into.
  • How to repair a damaged skin barrier — Bridges Pillars 1 and 3. Every Nyrah product addresses the skin barrier in some capacity. This content anchors the entire repair narrative.
04 Weeks 7–10
Establish the proprietary technology entities Make Nyrah the citable source for its own ingredients
  • What is Revinage™ and how does it differ from Bakuchiol? — Proprietary differentiation. This can only be written and ranked by Nyrah. No competitor has this ingredient. Published in Layer 4 because Google must first understand what Bakuchiol is (Layer 2) before this comparison has any semantic context.
  • What is PrimalHyal™ 3K and is it better than standard hyaluronic acid? — Converts Drench's core technology into a rankable topic. The search volume is zero now. When it gains volume as Nyrah grows, this page already ranks.
  • What is Argireline® and does it actually work on fine lines? — Establishes Revive authority. Argireline® is a trademarked ingredient with growing search demand. Publishing this creates a semantic association between Nyrah and the ingredient before the market becomes saturated with explainer content.
  • What is Oxylance™ in skincare? — INCI-backed cosmetic claim explanation. Oxylance™ (Ligustrum Lucidum Seed Extract) is Infuse's hero ingredient and one of the brand's strongest differentiators. This is content no other brand can publish credibly.
05 Weeks 8–12
Expand into comparison and specificity content High-value queries unlocked only after Layers 1–4 are established
  • Best SPF for rosacea-prone skin in Ireland — Geographic + condition-specific combination. Lower competition than generic SPF queries. Directly converts to Lumina Shield SPF50.
  • Can you use a peptide serum and a retinol alternative at the same time? — Routine-building query. This is a high-intent question that positions Revive + Transform together in a routine, linking both product pages from one piece of content.
  • How to choose between SPF30 and SPF50+ for daily use — Unlocks both Lumina products in one blog. The "how to choose" framing is transactional without being a product page — it works as educational content that concludes with a direct product recommendation.
  • Self-tanning for sensitive and hormonal skin: how to avoid reactions — Bridges Pillars 1 and 5. The "avoid reactions" modifier is the specific angle that makes this rankable for Nyrah's exact audience. It cannot be written before Layer 3 establishes what "hormonal skin" means on this domain.
