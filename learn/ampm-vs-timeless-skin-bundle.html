The key difference in plain English

The Complete AM/PM Routine is the full meal — every product Nyrah makes in one box. It covers all skin types and all concerns.

The Timeless Skin Bundle is the targeted meal — built specifically for aging skin. It swaps the daytime moisturiser for an SPF, and focuses everything on fine lines, firmness, and overnight repair.

5 of 6 products overlap. The real difference is Infuse + Tonic out, Lumina SPF in. One is broader, one is sharper.

Answer these questions to find which bundle makes more sense for your skin and your goals.

My skin is dry, sensitive, and reactive — but I'm not particularly concerned about aging or fine lines yet.
Complete AM/PM Routine

The Complete AM/PM Routine is the better fit. It includes Infuse — the daytime moisturiser — which is specifically designed for sensitive, reactive skin with Niacinamide and barrier-supporting ingredients. It also includes Tonic which calms irritation throughout the day. The Timeless Bundle skips both of these and focuses its energy on anti-ageing actives you don't yet need.

I'm going through perimenopause. My skin feels thinner, drier, and I'm noticing fine lines and dullness for the first time.
Timeless Skin Bundle

This is exactly who the Timeless Skin Bundle was built for. Every product in it was chosen with hormonal skin changes in mind. Transform addresses fine lines and cell renewal. Absorb provides deep overnight nourishment for thinning skin. And the Lumina SPF protects against UV-related collagen breakdown — the biggest cause of premature aging. This bundle treats the cause, not just the symptoms.

I want the most complete Nyrah experience and I'm happy to invest in the full system.
Complete AM/PM Routine

The Complete AM/PM Routine gives you all 7 products — including Infuse and Tonic which the Timeless Bundle does not have. It is the most thorough version of the Nyrah routine and covers every step morning and night without gaps. Note: it does not include an SPF, so you would need to add a Lumina SPF separately for full daytime protection.

I already have an SPF I love and I just want a complete skincare routine without it.
Complete AM/PM Routine

The Complete AM/PM Routine is the better choice here. It does not include an SPF, which means you are not paying for something you already have. You get all 7 products including Infuse and Tonic which the Timeless Bundle skips. Apply your existing SPF as the last step of the AM routine after Infuse.

I have rosacea or eczema-prone skin and I am worried about reactions.
Either — start with the Trial Pack first

Both bundles are designed to be safe for sensitive, rosacea, and eczema-prone skin. However, for reactive skin, the safest approach is to start with the Nyrah Trial Pack first — it contains smaller sizes of the core products so you can test each one on your skin before committing to a full-sized bundle. Once you know your skin responds well, choose based on your age and primary concern.

I want to fight aging and I also want daily sun protection in one system.
Timeless Skin Bundle

The Timeless Skin Bundle is the only Nyrah bundle that includes SPF as part of the routine. It gives you the anti-ageing actives (Transform, Absorb, Glisten) and the UV protection (Lumina SPF30 or SPF50+) in a single system. Dermatologists agree that SPF is the single most effective anti-ageing step available — this bundle treats it that way.

What the ingredient differences actually mean

Astaxanthin appears only in the Timeless Bundle via Lumina SPF. It is stated as being 6,000× more potent than Vitamin C as an antioxidant. This is the single most powerful anti-ageing ingredient across both bundles.

Niacinamide and Liquorice Root appear only in the Complete AM/PM Routine via Infuse. Both are specifically effective for redness reduction and brightening — making the Complete Routine stronger for reactive or rosacea-prone skin.

Spirulina appears in Glisten only in the Timeless Bundle version — it is absent from the Complete Routine version of the same product. This may indicate a formulation difference between bundles. Worth verifying with the brand.

Bakuchiol and Bidens Pilosa are in both — these are the primary anti-ageing actives in Transform and are present regardless of which bundle you choose.
