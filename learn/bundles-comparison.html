The Master Logic Behind Bundle Design

Undress is always there. Every routine starts with a clean surface. Non-negotiable.

Drench is excluded when Salicylic Acid conflicts with the bundle positioning. Rosacea and acne bundles already have it (no — wait — rosacea DOES include Drench; acne and brightening do not). The rule: if the bundle is built around "no harsh actives," Drench goes out.

Transform is excluded when fragrance or irritation is a clinical concern. Rosacea excludes it for essential oil triggers. Daily Routine excludes it as an advanced step for a foundational set.

Infuse is Fragrance-Free in every condition bundle except Bright & Even. Fragrance is a trigger for rosacea, acne, and sensitive/dry skin. The brightening bundle is the exception — and no reason is given on the page.

SPF is excluded from the two broadest sets (Complete AM/PM and Daily Routine) where customers choose their SPF separately. It is included in all five condition bundles because UV is a direct driver of each condition.

Absorb is a premium overnight product — it appears only where overnight deep repair is the core function (Timeless, Deep Hydration) or as the most comprehensive option (Complete AM/PM).
