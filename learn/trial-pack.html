The 5 problems it solves

These are stated word for word on the page. Knowing them helps you have better conversations with customers.

Problem 1
Skin that reacts to everything
Fragrance-free options, no harsh actives, no retinoids, no hormone-active botanicals. Formulated specifically for skin that has had reactions to other brands.
Without this: more failed products, more money wasted, more reactions.

Redness, burning, tingling, flare-ups
Niacinamide in Infuse, Aloe Vera and Chamomile in Tonic, anti-inflammatory actives in Glisten. The formulations are built around calming reactive skin, not ignoring it.
Without this: continuing to use products that make symptoms worse.
Problem 3
Hormonal breakouts and congestion
The routine is specifically built for hormonal skin. Non-comedogenic, no pore-blocking ingredients. The Trial Pack includes the same products as the Hormonal Breakout Bundle.
Without this: products that address one issue while worsening another.
Problem 4
Dehydration, dullness, uneven tone
Drench (triple molecular HA) addresses dehydration at three depths. Glisten (peptides + antioxidants) addresses dullness and tone. Together they are the visible result by Day 7.
Without this: dehydration continuing to compound as skin ages.
Problem 5
Fine lines, tired-looking skin
Glisten peptides signal collagen production. Drench plumps fine lines with hydration. The combination of five products working together creates the visible firmness and freshness response.
Without this: surface-level products that don't address the underlying cause.

The €10 credit journey

This is the retention mechanic. Understanding it is essential for helping customers use it.

Step 1

Purchase the Trial Pack

Customer buys at €24.95. During checkout or after, they create a Nyrah account using the same email address used for the purchase. This is essential — the credit links to the purchase email.

Step 2

Credit available instantly

The €10 digital store credit appears in the customer's Nyrah account dashboard immediately upon account creation. No waiting, no delay, no separate process.

Step 3

Redeem on any full-size product

The credit is redeemable on any full-size product or accessory in the Nyrah range. Customer logs in, copies the code from their dashboard, and applies it at checkout. Can also be gifted to someone else by contacting customer service.

If something goes wrong

Credit not received

Customer should contact store@nyrahbeauty.com or message via Instagram/Facebook @nyrahbeauty. The €10 credit FAQ covers three scenarios: never received, need advice, or want to gift the credit.
