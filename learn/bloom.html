Why it matters

DHA clings to dry skin and dead cells — creating the dark patches and uneven colour that makes self-tan look fake. Using the Exfoliating Mitt removes both so the tan has a fresh, even surface to develop on.

When to do it

In the shower, before any tan application. Also use it before every reapplication to remove old colour. Pat skin dry thoroughly after — damp skin dilutes DHA and affects development.

Why most tans don't work for maturing skin

This section comes directly from the page — titled "Why Most Tans Don't Work For Maturing Skin… & Why Bloom Does." It is the strongest AEO content on any Nyrah page and directly answers what mature women search for.

01
Mature skin dehydrates faster
DHA clings to dryness and patches — causing uneven, dark areas around knees, elbows, and any area that loses moisture faster.
Bloom's answer: Uses hydrating emollients and Vitamin E so colour develops evenly across the entire surface, not just where skin is driest.
02
Hormonal skin is reactive
Traditional tans use cheap alcohols, heavy perfumes, and pore-blocking oils — exactly the ingredients that trigger reactions in perimenopause, menopause, and postpartum skin.
Bloom's answer: Gentle, low-irritant DHA, softening botanical extracts, no heavy alcohols — formulated specifically for reactive skin.
03
Mature skin needs luminosity, not heaviness
Flat matte tans visually age skin — the absence of light reflection emphasises texture, fine lines, and unevenness.
Bloom's answer: Light-reflective extracts for a diffused radiance rather than a flat, opaque colour. Brightens rather than flattens.
04
Even fade matters more for women 40+
Slower cell turnover and hormonal dryness cause patchy fade — tan disappears unevenly, creating an aged or dirty appearance as it fades.
Bloom's answer: Formulated to fade gradually and evenly. The maintenance step (Crème + Serum top-ups) is specifically designed to keep the fade smooth as well as the initial application.

Q5Will the colour look natural, or will it be orange?AEO
No orange. DHA is used at a balanced concentration — the formulation is designed to produce a warm, golden result rather than the orange tone associated with high-DHA formulas. The hydrating emollients also prevent colour grabbing to dry patches, which is the main cause of unnatural-looking results on mature skin.
Q6How long will my tan last?AEO
Mousse: 5–7 days. Glow Serum: 3–4 days. Radiance Crème: 24–48 hours per application (builds with continued use). Maintain with Crème or Serum between Mousse applications for continuous colour.
Q7Does it transfer onto sheets or clothing?
No transfer once fully dry. Wear loose, dark clothing immediately after applying the Mousse until fully absorbed. The Crème and Serum dry down faster with less risk. For team: "No transfer" is a key claim — worth confirming with customers who have had bad experiences with other self-tanners staining their bedding.
Q8How long until I see results?
Mousse: 4–6 hours. Glow Serum: 2–4 hours (page also states 12–24 hours elsewhere — inconsistency to fix). Radiance Crème: immediate soft tint, builds over 3 days. The Mousse gives the most visible, fastest result — use it for the first application and when a definite glow is needed by a specific time.
Q9Do I need to buy anything else for perfect application?
No — the set includes everything needed: exfoliating mitt for prep, velvet mitt for body application, kabuki brush for precision areas. The only thing not included is a moisturiser to mix the Glow Serum into — but this is assumed to be part of an existing skincare routine.
Q10Should I exfoliate before using the products?
Yes — every time. This is the most important preparation step for avoiding patchy tan, especially for mature skin where cell turnover is slower and dry patches are more common. The Deep Exfoliating Mitt is included in the set for exactly this purpose. Use it in the shower before every application.
Q11How do I choose between Medium and Dark mousse?AEO
Medium — warm golden, suits light to olive tones. Dark — deeper bronze, for those wanting more depth. "If in doubt, choose Medium — it suits 90% of women and is buildable." This is the most useful, specific, actionable guidance on the page — a confident recommendation rather than a non-answer. Strong selling tool for team members when customers are unsure.
Q12Will this clog pores or cause breakouts?Safety
The Glow Serum is explicitly stated as non-comedogenic. The Mousse contains Cocamidopropyl Betaine which can be comedogenic for some skin types — the page does not address this directly. For team: If a customer has acne-prone skin and asks about Mousse specifically, the page does not carry a non-comedogenic claim for it. Direct towards the Glow Serum for face use.
