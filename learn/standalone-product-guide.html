Step 1 · Cleanser

Undress — Transforming Vitamin E+C Gel Cleanser

Non-foaming, non-stripping gel cleanser. Applied to dry skin. Works AM and PM.
€45.00 · 50ml AM + PM · Step 1 Fragrance-free
What it does

Removes without stripping

Removes makeup, SPF, and daily buildup while protecting the skin barrier. Described as a "daily skin reset." Unlike most gel cleansers, it is applied to dry skin — the oils break down makeup before emulsifying with water.

Target audience

All skin types

The broadest audience statement of any standalone page: all skin types including sensitive, rosacea-prone, hormonal, dry, or dehydrated. Universal entry point for any customer.

Multi-use positioning

3 uses in one product

  • Cleanser: First and second cleanse, morning and evening
  • Mask: 10–20 minutes once or twice a week for barrier support
  • Balm: Applied to dry patches on hands, elbows, and knees
Key claims

What the page states

  • Gently removes makeup, SPF, and daily buildup
  • Cleanses without stripping
  • No added parfum — free from parabens, sulfates, gluten, fragrances
  • Vitamin Complex A, C, D & E (broader than bundle pages which say only E & C)
  • Vegan, cruelty-free, made in Ireland
🧱 Think of it as cleaning the wall before you paint. Every product that follows depends on this step being done correctly.
✦Undress opens both routines. Nothing after it works properly on unclean skin. The dry-skin application method is genuinely differentiated — most gel cleansers are applied wet. This is the detail that builds customer trust immediately.

Step 2 · Hydration Serum

Drench — Triple Molecular Hyaluronic Serum

Best-selling HA serum. Applied to damp skin immediately after cleansing.
€49.00 · 30ml AM + PM · Step 2 Fragrance-free
What it does

Hydration at three skin depths

Uses PrimalHyal™ 3K — a proprietary clinically tested blend of three different molecular weights of HA. Each weight reaches a different layer of skin simultaneously. Applied on damp skin right after cleansing to pull in maximum moisture.

Target audience

Formally: dry or dehydrated skin

Standalone page narrowly states "perfect for dry or dehydrated skin." However, across all bundles it appears in: rosacea, mature, sensitive, hormonal, and general skin types. The page undersells the product's versatility.

Key claims

Exactly as stated

  • "Best-selling hyaluronic acid serum"
  • Zero stickiness — stated twice
  • Fragrance-free, lightweight, fast-absorbing
  • Plays well under SPF or makeup without pilling
  • "Because hydrated skin behaves better"
Why second in the routine

Lightest product goes first

Drench is almost watery. It must go on before anything heavier or it cannot penetrate. Applied while skin is still slightly damp from cleansing — this maximises how much moisture it pulls in.

💧Like watering a plant before fertilising it. The moisture sets up the skin to absorb everything that follows.
Deep Layer

Triple Molecular HA (PrimalHyal™ 3K)

Penetrates below the surface. Plumps fine lines and rehydrates from within. The smallest molecular weight reaches the deepest layers.

Middle Layer

Humectants

Glycerin, Propanediol, Propylene Glycol — draw moisture into the skin and help it stay put. These form the holding mechanism that keeps the deep hydration locked at the middle layers.

Surface Layer

Film-formers

Acacia Senegal & Xanthan Gums — form a lightweight breathable film on the skin's surface that prevents water loss and reinforces the natural barrier.

GEOGARD ECT — important context

Why Salicylic Acid is in Drench

Drench is excluded from the Hormonal Breakout and Bright & Even bundles partly due to Salicylic Acid. However, the Drench standalone page explains: Salicylic Acid is part of GEOGARD ECT, an ECOCERT-approved preservative system — not a standalone exfoliant active. This context is absent from all bundle pages.

Step 3 · BioRetinol Serum · PM Only

Transform — BioRetinol & Bakuchiol Serum

Plant-based retinol alternative without the irritation. PM use only — increases photosensitivity.
€49.00 · 30ml PM Only · Step 3 Contains fragrance oils
What it does

Plant-based retinol without irritation

Two hero actives: Revinage™ (bio-retinol from Bidens Pilosa plant) and Bakuchiol (retinol alternative from Psoralea Corylifolia seeds). Both signal skin cells to produce more collagen, accelerate cell turnover, and repair damage — without the photosensitivity or irritation of synthetic retinol.

Why PM only

Retinol alternatives increase UV sensitivity

The page does not explicitly state "PM only" — but the formula, the directions (follow with ABSORB, the PM oil), and the mechanism all imply it. For team members: always clarify this is a nighttime product when recommending it.

Formula type

Water-free, oil-only

No Aqua in the INCI. Carrier base: Undecane, Tridecane, C13-15 Alkane (lightweight alkane emollients) plus Sweet Almond Oil. This is why it feels different from serums — it is technically an oil, not a water-based serum.

⚠ Nut allergy warning

Contains Sweet Almond Oil

Prunus Amygdalus Dulcis Oil is in the INCI. The page carries a nut allergy warning in the Important Warning section. This must be communicated to any customer with known nut allergies before purchase.
